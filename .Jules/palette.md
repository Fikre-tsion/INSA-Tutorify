## 2025-05-18 - Mobile Navigation Icon Button Accessibility
**Learning:** Icon-only navigation buttons (`#open-menu-btn` and `#close-menu-btn`) lacked accessible names, making them unannounced or confusing for screen reader users.
**Action:** Always ensure icon-only toggle buttons have explicit `aria-label` attributes (`aria-label="Open navigation menu"` / `aria-label="Close navigation menu"`) across all page templates.
