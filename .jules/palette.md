## 2025-05-15 - Icon-only buttons accessibility
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons without `aria-label` are invisible to screen readers, making navigation and interaction impossible for visually impaired users.
**Action:** Always provide descriptive `aria-label` to buttons and links that contain only icons.

## 2025-05-15 - Form feedback and loading states
**Learning:** Users experience anxiety when clicking a submit button and seeing no immediate feedback. A loading spinner and a clear success message improve perceived performance and trust.
**Action:** Implement loading indicators on buttons for asynchronous operations and provide clear, non-intrusive success feedback.
